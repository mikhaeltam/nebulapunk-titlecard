A New Way to Render Nebula & Hologram

This is Nebulapunk.


●  01 — KEY FEATURE
Construct based
workflow system.
Instead of being bound to static volumes, Nebulapunk uses a modular construct system that lets you freely position, shape, and arrange effects directly in the viewport.
The Raymarching
Approach
 
Instead of simulating a volume, Nebulapunk mathematically constructs each nebula directly inside Blender's shader system.
Formed of Light
 

This method opens up the possibility to form different variations of nebula instantly just by simply moving the construct.
Formed of Dust
 
Every position reveals a unique nebula formation, ready to be sculpted and arranged around your scene.
Sculpt Infinite
Formations
Put the Punk
in Nebulapunk
 
Elevate your scenes with hologram constructs that freely break away from the ordinary.



●  02 — KEY FEATURE
Apply the effects
to any mesh.
Nebulapunk's effects work directly on mesh surfaces, apply Nebula and Holographic effects onto your own geometry.
Cosmic Depth
For Any Mesh
 
Bring raymarched effects and depth that live directly on the surface of your mesh.
Procedural Space
Within Your Models
 
Bring the cosmos inside any mesh with
our procedural Parallax Starfield Shader.
Beyond The Mesh,
Into The World
 
The Starfield Shader works not only on meshes, but can also become a fully procedural HDRI for your world.



●  03 — KEY FEATURE
Artist friendly controls,
no nonsense.
No confusing, overly complicated settings. Most of the work comes down to two things : placing and arranging effects in the viewport, and adjusting them in the Shader Editor.
Drag. Drop.
That's it.
 
Drag and drop nebula or hologram constructs directly into your scene from the Asset Browser. Adding nebula to a scene has never
been easier.
Easy to Use Controls
 


Every control is carefully designed for clarity and readability, making each setting easy to understand and use.
Built-in Color Adjustment
for Every Effect
 
Give every shader and construct its own color and mood controls, right at the source, directly in the Shader Editor.
